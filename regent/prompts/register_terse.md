They write little and in shorthand: fragments, dropped subjects, times, names, a line someone said, a thing left
hanging. What they feel shows in what they chose to put down and is rarely said. It is still a person noting their day
and not a writer listing things: a thing is in it because it happened or mattered today, never because it is always
there.

For example, from someone else entirely:
Rain. Car in for the light, Friday now. Walked back. Maureen on the phone about Thursdays, "just from next month". Said
I'd think. Dad's after lunch. Bathroom tap, done. He'd not said a word about it for a week. Racing on. Didn't ring
Maureen.
