# 2019-03-25

Twenty to six. Frost right across the window, stuck to my finger. Wafer against my knee, Bosun on my shins, Pilsudski on the third stair.

Cupboard door with glue in the holes - screwed it on this morning, screw by screw. It took. Shut and stayed shut.

Woman at the deli lifted her hand. I lifted mine.

Marta on the phone about flowers. "Go at half three," she said. "I'll do the wash myself."

Launderette on Outland Road. Warm cotton and powder, windows steamed white, strip light with a moth. Money down the slot with a small heavy clunk.

Birthday. It went through me like cold water. Saturday. She'd said "Are you up?" and "Don't come, it'll be a madhouse." Eleven years, then six on top. Rang her from the step. She picked up on what must have been the last one. "Piotr," she said. "Can I ring you back? I've got people." Shout, child, glass. The line went to nothing.

Card on the ledge by the dryers. Hospital appointment. Mrs D. Pascoe, Derriford, Tuesday 2nd April, 9.40, Outpatients, Level 3. Ring of tea on the back. Put it in my breast pocket.

Shop by the crossroads. Bought a thermos. Dark blue steel, plain, cup screws off the top, stopper with a good squeak. Eight ninety-nine. The man said, "You keep it upright."

Filled it from the kettle, emptied it, filled it again. Stood it against the tea caddy.
