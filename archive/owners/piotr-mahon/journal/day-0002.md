# 2019-03-05

Clear head two days running. Cupboard open again. Got the screwdriver. Bottom hinge gone to crumbs. Father's method: six Swan Vestas, wood glue, matched heads snapped into the holes, Stanley knife. Left door on worktop, told myself twenty-four hours.

Barry next door with hedge trimmer. Petrol, high whine. Bits of green flying over the fence.

Compost bag split in the hall. Black crumb over tiles, mat, dog's water. Swept it back, put it in the blue crate. Split side to wall.

Vet rang. Chloe. Bosun Monday 11 instead of Thursday 4. Text from Roisin: someone's looking at the gutters. Ladder still on roof rack from last time.

Old man came about his wife. Looked down at my shoes. You keep them nice. I said they were my father's last, which wasn't true.
