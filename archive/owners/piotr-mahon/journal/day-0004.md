# 2019-03-07

Fog in. House sounds wrapped. Thermos from my father—green steel, dent in base. Dropped it off a scaffold in 1981. Been saying it was the stopper. Stopper was fine. Boiled kettle, filled to top. Palm test: just warm. Should be cold as a step if the vacuum held. Warm right through. The dent. Dying by inches.

Dog's nose on back door crack. Opened it. Fog at the knees. He stopped. Smelled something. Man's cough from the other side of the fence. Bosun skidded back in on the lino.

Took him out anyway. Walked the other way, away from Plain, away from yard. Central Park thirty yards of visibility. Trees black and wet. Carried him up Alma Road. Breath cold on my neck.

Washed hearse in fog, which is a mistake. Chamois cold and heavy. Stairwell foul. Alan on phone above, voice rising at ends of sentences. Ate the sausage roll cold. Flask tepid, bit grey, taste of metal.

Nursing home at Efford. Small woman, very light. Corridor gravy and floor polish.
