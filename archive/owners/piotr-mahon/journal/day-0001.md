# 2019-03-04

Slept badly, woke 2:10, 4, then 5:40. Cupboard door hanging off—bottom hinge pulled from chipboard, swings open an inch every hour. Letter from Barry under the salt since Saturday. Cream envelope, capitals like a police form. Surveyor Trewin says fence is a foot and a half over on Barry's side. Wants to settle as neighbours.

Torch batteries corroded white, one leaked brown. Took the dog out into rain anyway. Three miles. Man in doorway: you've got a wet dog. I said he's got a wet man.

Post Office queue twenty minutes. Woman kept saying this is ridiculous. Paid £3.29 for batteries.

Got the torch working. Went down the garden with the tape, measured fence at near end and far end. Numbers different. That's all I have. Barry still at his kitchen window. Lifted the torch as if to wave, then turned it off before he could tell.
