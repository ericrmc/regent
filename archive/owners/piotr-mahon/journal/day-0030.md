# 2019-04-02

Window open already. Bosun flat on lino like frog. Toast, end of jar, thin smear, margarine. That jar she wrapped in pastry string—left it at deli counter. Exactly what I do now.

Machine seal black. Split in two places pencil-wide. Did gaffer tape anyway. Hot wash. Brown water tongue over edge, across lino, reached beer mat. Turned it off. Relief surprised me. Launderette now, Plain, pound coins.

Funeral Efford. Pascoe eighty-eight. Seven chapel, one wheelchair. Sun bright on crematorium lawn. Daffodils too yellow. Marta conducting. Light coffin. Kieran opposite, stepping exactly on beat. Wheelchair man, son I think, hat on knee rubbed brim round. "Well, Dad" when we came past. Blackbird in shrubs wouldn't stop.

Green Thermos, dent in base, father's or mother's, kept tea hot since before Voisey's. Half twelve—no steam, temperature of bath, cool as milk. Done three times this week. Rattle in it beetle in matchbox. Drank standing by bins. Kieran with cup from kettle brown as boot. Offered. No. Sharp. Went careful with door.

Physio. Text said Thursday. Didn't reply YES so stayed for today. Form, body drawing, shade where it hurts. Shaded neck. Ticked RIGHT side without reading. Gemma: it's the left stiff. Crossed out, initialed G.T. Line sits in my head now. Word RIGHT over my answer.

Twenty past three. Drove to Plym bridge, towpath. Trees barely leafed. Pale green. Two men with spaniel. Tall: "sixty-seven sixty-eight that's your body. Down sixty-three thin dry ferments nothing drink water with a hat on. Hold it up leave sugars got weight. One number. Hold nerve one number one hour." Other: wife won't have pan in house. "Shed."

Bench by old bridge. Foil balloon tied arm. Silver. Nine. Deflated. Nodding.

Sunday thirty-first. Roisin's birthday. Fifty-two, no fifty-one. Doesn't matter. Launderette folding table thinking duvet and Stoke machine. Hospital call. Never thought date. Two days. Cross in pencil calendar hall for years. After split went on four years. Some year didn't. Didn't notice.

Rang. Didn't know I meant to. Her voice clatter. "Piotr?" door opening crack. "About Sunday that I'd—" "Hang on go outside." Man's voice. Shape of question. "It's only"—trees took it. Call Failed. Picture of path.

Didn't ring back.
