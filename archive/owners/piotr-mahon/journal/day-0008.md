# 2019-03-11

Awake since three with rain on the back window. Walked the Dawes bike home from the bottom of the hill—two hours, no pump, no tape. Bent link in the shed drawer.

Voisey's: two removals by ten. Marta with the leave form, yellow one. Appointment noted: 2 April 2.10. Ears. Audiology. A girl I taught to fold a sheet now knows.

Stairwell at lunch under the flickering light. Huntley & Palmers tin on the sill—been there longer than I have. Brass screws, cup hooks, washers in twisted newspaper. Found the one I needed: thirty-two, half-inch. Stood with my hand flat on the picture of the ladies in the carriage.

Radio coming up from the garage about a catchment, lag between rainfall and peak flow. Denny came down wet. "Mother of God, have you seen the yard?" Mercedes under a sheet. Hearse gleaming. The Plain had become a grey river.

Rain still going at four. Took the umbrella from the bus stop—black, wooden handle. Left it on the back seat because somebody would want it.

Home. Bosun on my feet, stiff in the hindquarters, pressing into my legs. Pilsudski on the third stair. Neither of us gave way. Picked the dog up.
