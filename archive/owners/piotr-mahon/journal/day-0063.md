# day 63

Day 63, 21 Sept. Up 5:40, tea, thumb on the chip. Bosun bottom stair, Pilsudski third, carried him up and said nothing, as usual. Van started first go, small mercy. Removal from Efford at nine, quiet house, brought a chair without being asked. Sausage roll in the van, cold. Marta at the hearse again, Thursday, Kieran. Said I know. Still haven't. Wafer on the laptop when I sat down. Read the code, one file. Thought of Halina's name spelled the old way on the card. Landing tonight, maybe. Nula Sunday, bus 34.
