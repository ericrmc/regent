# 2019-03-24

Twenty to six. Fog. Window a blank. Not dark, not light. Hand against the glass. Bosun on shins. Wafer on pillow with her back to me. Pilsudski on the third stair, grey shape in grey.

Filled the thermos with boiling water first, to warm it. Cool already. Not cold, worse than cold. Stood watching the steam. Took it anyway—old green one my father had in his tin box for the dockyard, dented at the shoulder. Shook it by my ear. Small loose glassy noise of something gone in the wall of it. Shook it before. Put it on the side by the bread bin.

Road to Mutley all headlamps and no shapes. Walked. Octavia had frost on it. Fog thick white that doesn't move, holds sound close. Woman's heels forty yards away seemed in my own pocket. Wet on the eyelashes.

Deli shut, grille down. Stood a moment, thought of the jar with its smear at the bottom. Went on.

Yard empty. Let myself in by the side door. Smelled of cold rubber and wax and faint sweet chemical smell from the prep room below. Back stairs on Sunday—different place. No feet. No phone.

Strip light on middle landing did its flicker. Stood under it. Fog got in even there. Concrete beaded. Fire door when I shouldered it came off its frame with the usual shudder. Stood holding it. Thought I might see about that. Not today.

Hearse first. Leather on chrome, long cloth down the flanks, black paint coming up under wet like a pond. Wheels with small brush, all the spokes, tyre walls. Fog settled on roof as fast as I dried it. Polishing a thing that was being un-polished. Gave it up around the bonnet. Did the inside instead—rollers in back, little brass fittings, floor. Nobody about. Bus somewhere sounding like it was in another postcode.

Eleven. Sat on middle landing with sausage roll from Greggs, still warm from the bag. Thermos on the step. Poured tea into cup. Temperature of a cup of tea that has been thought about. Drank it anyway. Sausage roll made flakes down my front. Picked them off one at a time, put them in the bag.

Smell in that stairwell in fog I've never got used to. Wet and cream paint and something that is not in either.

Thought about Wednesday. Ten past four. Said it once to the light.

Two Mercedes. Made a bad job of the second. Left it.

Did not go home. By one it was clear that at three the phone would go in the hall. Standing there with my hands full of nothing. Turned left out of the yard instead of right.

Walked. Down through Mutley and Greenbank, along by the station and down towards the Hoe. Everything shut and dripping. Couldn't see the Sound. Heard it. Foghorn at the Breakwater gave its long low note every half minute. Whole city seemed to lean toward it. Hoe—grass drowned. Smeaton's Tower a red and white stump going up into nothing. Few people. Man with a Labrador in a coat. Two lads on bikes out of the whiteness with no lights and gone.

Stood at the rail where the Sound should be. Grey and white, the same, top and bottom, no line. Thought I could stand there and be at the end of a place.

Barbican. Fish market steps. Round again. By time my feet on the road home had been walking four hours. Neck pulling. Soles on fire. Thermos knocked at my hip like a small dead thing. Twice nearly threw it in a bin. Twice didn't. Couldn't have said why except the bin was wet and it was his.

Home ten to six. Hall dark. Phone on its shelf by the pig, looked like nothing had happened. Didn't check. Didn't look at it. Put my hand on the pig, on the brown envelope under it, and moved on.

Animals gathered in kitchen like a committee. Did the dishes, all four.

Took the cupboard door—hangs a quarter inch proud. Got the screwdriver from the drawer, box of matches from over the cooker. Trick is the hole's gone soft, screw won't bite. Break two, three matches, glue them in, let them dry. Got the door down, laid it on the counter. Dug out old screw holes, blew the dust. Yellow of old glue in one of them. Thing I do and forget I do.

Broke the matches. Sulphur head smell, small and sharp. Pushed splinters in with the flat of my thumbnail. Glue went on white and cold. Stood with the door in my two hands like a man holding a tray, waiting for it to take. Wafer watched from the fridge.
