# 2019-04-11

Still and bright, frost only where the shade was. Tea, toast, Bosun out and back. Asked Alan first thing for Thursday afternoon, the physio, just the two hours. He said Mrs Adair's the Thursday, Piotr, we need every pair of hands. I said I'm not a pair of hands, I've a neck. He looked at his diary like it might change. Refused. Marta stayed out of it, which I noticed.

Keys after that. The yard ring has eleven on it and I know what seven open. Brass one with red tape, nobody knows. Kept turning it in my pocket all morning until the pocket was warm and I wanted to throw the lot in the Sound.

Took the van up to the reservoir path at twelve and sat on the bench an hour. Sausage roll, cold. Walkers with sticks, no one talking. Someone had creosoted the fence by the car park and the smell came up warm off the boards. Con's funeral, spring 2003, our neighbour doing his fence that same week, hammering through the hymn practice. Exactly that. Sat with it.

Then remembered Roisin's birthday. Yesterday. Rang from the bench, four rings, her machine. Said happy birthday, sorry, I'll bring something Saturday. Then I said the boiler and it cut off. Didn't ring back.
