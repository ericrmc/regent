# 2019-04-06

Allotment early. Sun on the shed, then hail out of nothing, rattling on the water butt lid. Stood in the shed door with the bootlace in my hand until it passed. Then wind, then warm again. Reg said it would do that and I said thanks, Reg.

Gave him back his secateurs. Three weeks I've had them. He looked at the blades and said I'd oiled them, which I hadn't, it's the beetroot damp. He didn't say more. Gave me a bag of last year's seed potatoes, soft and sprouting.

On the way home I stopped at the churchyard gate. Didn't go in. The card in the office is still F and I still don't trust my count.

Sat on the front step with my own secateurs, the rusty pair, and a rag. Under the rag, in the old tin, there was a photograph. Con on a staging in the dockyard, cap pushed back, squinting, a rope over his shoulder. Corner gone soft. Halina's writing on the back, one word in Polish I had to say out loud to read. Bosun put his chin on my boot.

Audiology card is still behind the magnet on the fridge. 2 April. Rang no one. Monday, maybe.
