# 2019-03-27

Twenty to six. Window open an inch. Must have been Wafer, or the latch gone. Bosun kicked the blanket off - he never does that. Pilsudski stretched out flat on the third stair, belly showing.

Tea. Toast at back door, no breath to see. Garden wet and steaming where sun caught the fence.

Machine blinking. Monday at eleven with Miss Adeyemi for Bosun. Ears and lump.

Went out no overcoat. Left without my trousers. Sleeves rolled by the Plain. Everyone walking slow with faces up. Man outside the bookmaker's had taken his shoes off.

Deli propped open with a crate. Coffee and cinnamon. Woman not in window. I went by.

Ten o'clock at the crematorium. Seventy-eight, big turnout, all hot in black. Dennis "Nice bit of weather for it." Took my hand in both. "Good man," and looked past me for someone else.

Market. Flower woman - "You're Voisey's, aren't you, the tall one who doesn't say. My mum went through you in ninety-eight. You did her hair." "You brushed it forward, and my auntie said she'd never had it like that alive."

Far end, tools. Bicycle pump. Brass, wooden handle dark from hands. Father's model. Two pounds. Bought it.

Tea van. Bicycle leaning on the railings - lady's with a basket and bell. Back tyre flat to the rim. Crouched, unscrewed the cap, pumped. Forty strokes. Arm knew. Woman came out with clementines. "That's mine." "Always down?" "It's always down. How much do I owe?" "Nothing." She pedalled away wobbling, bell once by accident. Stayed with pump in hand, smell of hot rubber on my fingers. Pleased. Two pounds, did what it said.

Secondhand books. Leather glove on a cover, bird's feet gripping. Read where it fell open - "The bird is flown at a weight, and the weight is the control, because a bird that is not hungry does not return." Somebody had underlined "not hungry" and put a small tick.

One o'clock back to yard. Sausage roll, blue thermos, pump on the stair. Tea hot through the stairwell. Strip light flickering. Kieran with the chamois. "That's a nice pump." "It's a good pump." "My bike's in the shed, back's going." "Bring it?" "Would you?" Said he would, then stopped.

Afternoon - laying out. Woman from Peverell, ninety-one, small, good face. Did her hair. Very fine. Nails painted pink, one thumb chipped. Looked for shoes on the chair. Slippers. Stood there with the comb.

Home by six. Light in the sky. Bosun at the back door with his blanket. Wafer through the flap. Pilsudski on the third stair, didn't move.

At seven took the washing in. Saw Barry in shirtsleeves. "Evening. Lovely." Against the back wall of the shed, under the guttering, where I'd seen them on Sunday, stood the loppers. Red handles gone dull. Blades out. Fur of rust. He saw where I was looking. Looked at shed, then at me. Saw him decide something. "That hedge is going to be the death of me." Nothing said. Put the sheet over my shoulder.

"Anything you want doing on your side, just say."

Went in. Heard his can go down. Thought - he hasn't said. Thought I hadn't either. Thing you don't ask for stays where it was put. Pump still on the hook. Went and got it, just to hold.
