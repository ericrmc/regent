# 2019-04-13

Rain at eight, then sun, then hail on the shed roof like thrown rice, then sun again. Wore the coat all morning and took it off twice. Letter still in the inside pocket. Dear Sirs, and then crossing out I refuse to accept. Twenty-eighth. Didn't take it out.

Dug the last of the potatoes in the wet. Halina's wooden spoon lives in the shed in a jam jar, worn flat on one side from forty years of her stirring the same pot against the same corner. I use it to scoop compost into the trays. Nobody knows that and Reg would have something to say.

He did have something to say anyway, over the wire with his flask. Heard Voisey's had a man round the yard Tuesday, suit, clipboard, walked the garage twice. Who told you, I said. A woman at his church whose brother does the gutters there. So, nothing. Reg nodded like it was something. I said nothing back and pulled a beetroot that was too small.

His radio was on, some doctor talking about putting people under. Depth goes by how they respond, he said, not by how much you give, because what works differs three times over between one person and the next. Then hail again and I lost the rest.

Rang Roisin from the gate, where it gets one bar. The boiler, I said. She said hang on, someone's at the door, and the line went. Stood holding it. Didn't ring back. Sink full of beetroot water, pink to the rim.

The list thing: one report said something about which line it points to and I thought, fine, whoever it is, get it right. I'd looked myself. It exists, one file, but I never saw it run.
