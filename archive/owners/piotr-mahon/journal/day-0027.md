# 2019-03-30

Twenty to six. Window white. Someone hung a sheet on the outside. Couldn't see fence or path or pear. Tea, thumb on the chip. Cupboard open its finger's width. Beer mat on the lino this morning - didn't pick it up. Stood with foot next to it.

Bosun wouldn't go out. Stood at back door, nose to the gap, whole body leaning away. "It's only fog." Looked at me like only the sea. Opened door a foot. Cold wet came in. Cut grass smell no business in March. Every sound near and flat. Car door somewhere. Gull over and over. Pilsudski came down off third stair to watch, sat on mat back to the dog.

Card on hall shelf. Put there Thursday night, sat under Derriford envelope two days. Blue one from newsagent, boat on it, inside written nothing. Roisin was fifty-five yesterday. Twenty-ninth. Know it as own postcode. Had it on my hand Wednesday - vet, rain, clipboard - gone. Stood with card in fingers, felt day go by behind me, whole of yesterday, her in it, Stoke, kitchen.

Took phone out of jacket. Crack across bottom corner, spider of glass. Keyed her number. Rang five times. "Hello?" His voice said something. Tap running. She said "Piotr?" turning from room. "I'm a day late." "Late for what?" Heard it herself. "Oh. Oh, love." Tap stopped. Other voice asked something low. She said "Hang on, it's just Piotr." Just. Said wouldn't keep her. Line full of nothing shut in. Beeping. Stood with phone to ear. Didn't ring again. Put card in jacket with boat pressed against lining.

Parcel meant seed potatoes. Fifteen pounds Charlotte and Pink Fir Apple from Devon. Card on mat - SORRY WE MISSED YOU. I was in from six. Didn't knock. Redelivery online.

Went to plot anyway. Walked. Could tell by sound where Plain was, where Peverell Park Road. Fog took buildings. Shutter going up. Spade on step. Woman said good morning from about a yard out of white nothing.

Bus. Lights on. Wipers for rain not falling. Six of us. All looked straight ahead.

Gate stuck unless you lift it. Lifting in wet like lifting dead man's arm. Track stones and standing water. Couldn't see past third plot. Shed came up like a boat. Bootlace holding it. Stood hand on wet felt. Didn't go in.

"You've no spuds in," said Reg from somewhere left. On his bucket at edge of bed. Waxed jacket. Flask in lap. Knitted hat. "They never do, not when you want them. Sit down, boy, you look like I've had you on a rope." Shoved flask at me. Tea with sugar and something else - brandy or memory of brandy. Drank and coughed. Went through like hot wire to belt. "That's medicine," he said.

Talking about bees. Two hives at daughter's over Yelverton. Sits by them. Hive loses queen - doesn't stop, makes new queens. Whole row at once - six eight ten - in cells at edge of comb. First one out stings the others in their beds, or workers do it. All of them. "All that work, all that royal jelly. Spent it before they knew which one was any good." He wiped flask mouth. "Waste, you'd say. But they can't know. So they do the lot." Wood pigeon in fog. Four notes and stop.

Did the leeks. Nothing else. Half a row since October. Lifted with fork. Earth came away black and cold and stinking sweetly. Knocked roots on boot. Laid them on the sink. Good while with a leek in each hand. White went on. Seemed come up out of ground rather than down.

Back at one - note through door. Not a card. Page from pad, folded twice. Woman at deli: "Piotr, I saw you go past Thursday in the wet. Didn't shout. Jar's behind the counter, it's the last of the batch, come for it tomorrow if you can't today." Never written me a note. Bought jam eleven years, didn't know she knew where I lived. Read in passage with coat on. Turned it over. Smaller writing: "Nobody should have toast with nothing on."

Kitchen too bright after. Leeks in sink. Cupboard door swung right out, wide open. Showed pipe and pan of cloths and bucket - everything. Looked at it. Meant to shut it. Got as far as beer mat. Knelt down. Knee went off like a shot. Stayed on lino, palm flat. Phone pressing into hip, crack digging in. Bosun at shoulder, breathed. Out in yard, somebody sawing slow. Thought Barry. Thought no, it's further - listened see if getting nearer or only...
