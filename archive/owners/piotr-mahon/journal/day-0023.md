# 2019-03-26

Twenty to six. No frost. Just wet, grey wet, streetlamp in it.

Bosun on shins, Wafer on pillow, Pilsudski on third stair with paws tucked in like a loaf.

New thermos filled while kettle loud, warmed, emptied, filled. Old green one - put in cupboard under sink behind bleach. Door shut. That pleased me.

Woman at deli in the door. "Piotr. I've a jar, when you want it. Come for it today if you can't tomorrow."

Dennis in early, black tie. Took both hands. "Piotr, Piotr, how are you keeping?" "Good man, good man," and let go.

Rang vet at ten. Wed 4.10 on back of my hand. Girl very bright. "Oh, Bosun, yes, the ears and the lump." Miss Adeyemi's been called to a horse. Not Wednesday afternoon. Monday at eleven? I said Monday had a funeral. "Tight." I said it might be. Then gave work number. She read it back wrong. I corrected it. She read it back wrong the same way.

Marta pencilled out Wednesday. Monday now. Small pause. I said it wasn't bereavement, it was a beagle. She laughed.

Removal from Stoke. Man of ninety in bed by window. Chair still warm. Son wanted to wash him. Shoes under the radiator - brown lace-ups polished to a mirror. Son said, "He'd have wanted the black ones."

On landing at noon. Thermos steam fogged glasses. Hot at noon. Burned my lip, didn't care. Eight ninety-nine sticker on the base. Picked at it with a nail, came off in a long strip.

Derriford card. Mrs D. Pascoe. Tuesday 2nd April, 9.40. Number on back, different biro, smudged. Read it four times.

Kieran on the stairs. Looked at the card in my hand, then away, politely.

Afternoon - laying out - woman eighty-three, good face. Nails painted pink, one thumb chipped. Looked for shoes on the chair. Slippers. Thought of the towpath, the hail, the compost. It was warm now. Never think.

Didn't take the bucket back. In Octavia boot with earth dried on it. Old man said it was the only one.

Home by six. Light still in sky. Eggs. Sat down. Bosun at back door, nose over sill. Wafer through the flap. Pilsudski on the third stair, didn't move.

Saw Barry at seven in shirtsleeves, can in one hand, weight on one leg, looking at his hedge. Behind him against the shed wall under the guttering stood the loppers. Red handles gone dull. Blades out. Fur of rust. He saw where I was looking. Looked at shed, then me. Saw him decide something. "That hedge is going to be the death of me." I said nothing. Put sheet over my shoulder.

"Anything you want doing on your side, just say."

Went in. Heard his can go down on the wall. Thought - he has not said. Thought I hadn't either. Thing you don't ask for stays where it was put.
