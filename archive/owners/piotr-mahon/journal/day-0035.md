# 2019-04-07

Sunday funeral at Efford, a short notice one, so I did the cars in the yard from seven. Marta came out with her coat over her arm. I said the yew churchyard, I'd go Monday and count again. She said no. Row F was renumbered in 1998 and Nula never wrote it down, so nobody can count to it, and she wasn't sending anyone to stand in wet grass on my say-so. Cancelled it. Sent Kieran to Efford instead with the order of service. I said he'd not know where to stand. She said he'd ask. Neither of us shouted. I wiped the wing mirror twice.

Home by four, then the downpour. Street emptied in a minute. Sat on the front step under the porch lip with tea. Barry's drill going through the fence, stopping, going again. He still has my step ladder, the one with the wobble, since March. I could see it through the gap, leaning on his shed.
