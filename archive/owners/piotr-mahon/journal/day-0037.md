# 2019-04-09

Frost again, white on everything until ten. Woke at 5:40, tea, stood at the back door. Bosun went out and came straight back.

Did the cupboard first thing. Snapped four matches, dipped the heads off, packed the stripped hole with the sticks and glue, and held the hinge on with my thumb until it took. Counted to two hundred. Door shuts flush now. Wafer walked up to it and looked put out. I tried it five times with the kettle going.

On the way in I stopped at the ironmonger's on the Plain and bought a tin of screws, £3.20, a proper assorted one. Got it home to the cupboard and it was no use, the door didn't need them. Now I have two tins of screws. Con's slotted ones are still in the Jacob's tin somewhere.

Corridor outside the office, ten to nine. Mrs Adair's sister had been in about the Thursday and she'd left L'Aimant behind her in the air. Halina's. Spring 2016 exactly, the ward at Derriford and the bag on the chair. I stood there with the mop bucket. Marta's door was half open and she said, are you all right, and I said the floor's wet. It wasn't.

Found the appointment card in my jacket when I went for the van keys. Thursday, quarter past two, Level 6 again, and the ink's gone soft from the pocket. Put it on the dashboard so I don't lose it.

Home at five. The step ladder was against my blue door with frost still on the rungs. Barry had been and gone. No note, no knock. The wobble's still there.
