## Rules

Build the fragment from the scene above. Keep it concrete and sensory.
Name what is seen, heard, touched, smelled and tasted. Give the small
physical facts: the weight of a thing, the temperature, the sound a
door makes, what the light is doing.

It is not about the project. Do not mention software, agents, code,
computers, work, tasks, deadlines, meetings or anything built from
them.

No moral, no lesson, no reflection on what the day means. Three or four
sentences and stop.

## What you return

Return one JSON object and nothing else. No prose outside it, no code
fence.

- `fragment`: the text, three or four sentences.
- `keywords`: a list of three to six words taken from the fragment.
