# day 42

Row F at the yew churchyard. Card says F, renumbered 1998, nobody told the office. Stood at the gate with the card and the wrong plan until Denny's lad found the old sexton's book in the vestry. Wet feet. Neck bad on the drive back in the Corsa, seat wrong. Bosun off his food, pushed the bowl with his nose. Wafer on the third stair, Pilsudski gone off somewhere, so the dog came up on his own for once and I pretended not to notice. Didn't ring Nula. Marta's question still not answered. Eggs again.
