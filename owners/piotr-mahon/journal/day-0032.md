# 2019-04-04

Clear and hard cold, light so sharp the chrome hurt. Tea in the lay-by on the hill road with the engine off. Thermos lukewarm again. Poured it on the verge.

Bucket back to the boatyard at ten. Taped the crack, silver, two inches, went round twice. The man turned it over and ran his thumb along the tape. "Didn't need doing." Then he gave me tea from a chipped enamel jug, black rim, blue gone to grey where the chip was. Stood in the cabin. Neither of us said anything about anything. Better than most conversations this week.

Marta at half eleven. Wanted Kieran and me at the churchyard with the yew to check a plot. The card in the office index still says row F. F was renumbered in 1998. Nula knew which cards were wrong. Never wrote it down, it was in her head. I said so. Marta: "Then write it down." I said I wasn't paid to write. She said that isn't what she asked. It wasn't about the card. Went on longer than the card. Then she cancelled the trip anyway and sent Kieran to Efford with the flowers. I washed the hearse twice.

The thing they're making to find the dead links in my notes still hasn't been run. Blocked, apparently. I looked at what it does with a renamed heading. It says the heading's gone, not what it's called now. Fair. That was always Nula's part.

Phone in my pocket all afternoon. Didn't take it out.

Bosun waiting on the hall carpet. Pilsudski on the third stair
