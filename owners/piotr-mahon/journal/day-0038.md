# 2019-04-10

Drizzle from first light, the kind that finds the gap at your collar. The garage door at the yard is stuck at half-mast again, up-and-over, one spring gone. Kieran and I lifted it between us and it screamed the whole way. I stood under it in the boots. Black, lace-ups, twenty-odd years and they have never once been comfortable. Left heel rubs, always has. Nula said a bearer's boots should be ugly and quiet. Mine are only ugly.

The green flask was cold by ten. Tea like pond water, no heat in it at all. The vacuum has gone. Shook it by my ear and it rattled. Brought it home and put it on the kitchen windowsill next to the spider plant. Not throwing it out. Not filling it either.

Made soup at six, chicken and dill, too much of it. Rang Roisin about the dentist and she'd a cold, so I took a tub round. She was in her dressing gown. Something knocking in the boiler cupboard while I stood in the hall. She said it's always done that. It hasn't.
