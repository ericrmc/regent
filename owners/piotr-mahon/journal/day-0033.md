# 2019-04-05

Churchyard with the yew at nine, on my own. Card in the index says F. Stood in the wet grass and counted from the wall, the way I think she did. Got a number I don't trust. Frost still in the yew's shadow, none anywhere else.

Bus to Devonport. Waited by the chemist. Someone's bicycle against the shelter, back tyre soft, flat to the rim. Nobody came for it in twenty minutes. Pressed the tyre with my thumb. Don't know why.

Nula in the chair with the blanket, hips bad, skin like paper on her hands. Smelled of Vick and toast. I asked her straight out which cards are wrong. Just the rows, I said, I'll write it. She looked at the window. "No." Then: "Go and stand in it, Piotr. I'm not doing it from a chair." That was all. Gave me a Rich Tea and I ate it.

Home. Rings note open, the family one still not written. Bosun on the hall carpet. Didn't lift him up the stairs for a while.
