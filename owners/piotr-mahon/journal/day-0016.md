# 2019-03-19

Window grey and wet, frost gone, sill dripping. Bosun on shins, Wafer on my chest. Pilsudski on the third step, tail hanging like a rope someone forgot.

New flask worked. It's ugly—colour of a fire bucket—but it holds heat.

Cupboard door hung true or not? Spirit level at Barry's since October. Went to the back garden. He was there in a fleece with a mug. "Piotr. You'll be after the level." Through the shed window I could see something long and yellow against a paint tin. White smear on it—from my ceiling, the landing.

Morning was Mrs Ackland, ninety-one, hospice. Daughter with a carrier bag—cardigan, lipstick. Brown lace-ups with a hole worn at the little toe. She said: "She wouldn't get them mended."

Marta on the stairs: "Are you all right for Thursday? I had you down for the Penhale." Forgotten. I said might be needed at Derriford Thursday. She looked at me one beat longer than she needed to.

Audiology rang. Thursday clinic cancelled, consultant off sick. Could they do 2nd April instead, ten to twelve? I said yes. Girl: "It's Mahone, is it?" "With the e." She laughed.

Garden centre—compost, forty litres. Lad with trolley: "The beetroot man." Bag split on the boot mat. He gave me another ten litres, wouldn't take money.

Towpath instead. Creek full and brown, houseboats, a heron on the mud. Sky went black in about a minute. Hail came—white pellets bouncing off the cinder, rattling on the houseboat roof like gravel. Ran to the boatyard hut with Bosun flat against my leg.

Sheet in the window there, laminated, corners curled, coffee ring across the bottom: "A ship under way gives way to a ship constrained by her draught, because manoeuvrability and not right of way decides who can act." Two arrows in a diagram. Someone had written in biro in the margin: "THIS MEANS YOU DEREK."

Bosun was shaking, ears wet through.

Washer test run. Drum turned—low wet grumble. One thump at the spin, then settled.

Eggs out of the pan with a fork, heel of bread. Standing at the cooker. Light on in Barry's kitchen across the fence.

Lorraine at the door at twenty to eight with a plate covered in foil. Lemon drizzle.
