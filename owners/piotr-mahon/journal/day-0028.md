# 2019-03-31

Twenty to six. Window ticking. Sleet small and hard, then gap, then rain - couldn't tell which listening to. Tea, thumb on the chip. Cupboard open its finger's width. Beer mat still on lino where left yesterday. Stepped over it.

Bosun's blanket had to go. Pilsudski sick on it in the night. Machine leaking from door seal since week before. Stuffed blanket and bed cover and two work shirts into bin bag. Went to launderette on Plain with bag over each shoulder. Sleet stinging good side of neck.

Steamed windows. KELLY written on glass with finger. Red lino mopped pale in walking lines. Heat at the door. Hot lint and powder and something under it like warm iron. Two machines going. Woman in dressing gown under mac sat with arms folded watching hers, in case. Put things in big one at end. Coins only if push them flat with thumb. Been like that since first came - with Roisin, machine at Stoke broken, she sent me down with a duvet. Don't think about that. Thought about it.

Man came in, talking to someone outside, still there. Mid thirties, hi-vis over hoodie, wet through at shoulders. Propped door with foot. "My brother does bridges. Inspects them. For the council, or was, now it's firm. Got this little hammer - goes along tapping it and listens. That's it. If note's dull, flat, that's a hole. Behind it. Could stand there all day - perfect, paint on, no rust - but it goes dull." "Get so you can hear it before you've thought about it." Let door go. Shook like dog.

Watched blanket go round. Bicycle chained to drainpipe, lady's dark green with basket. Back tyre flat as pancake. No one near it. Went out. "Slow puncture?" Forgot patches. Got pump out of Octavia. Long brass thing. Father's. Rigging shed, dockyard. Pumped and he held wheel. Tyre came up hard under my hands. "That'll go down again." Pumped harder. "You'll blow it." "Won't." He looked at me and tyre and said "Cheers, mate" and didn't ask anything else. Liked him.

Phone went while folding shirts. Cracked one, glass caught thumb. Text from Derriford. "Appointment 2nd April rescheduled owing to clinic change. New one Thursday 10.10. Reply YES to confirm or call to discuss." Read it standing at folding table. Thursday 10.10 - chapel to set, family at four. Tuesday was fixed. Tuesday on paper in envelope under hand on Level 6, hail coming. Didn't reply YES. Thought would go Tuesday anyway, with letter, stand at desk, let them tell me to my face. Not good plan. Like it.

Blanket came out hot and heavy. Carried it home against chest. Steamed under chin whole way up Outland Road.

One o'clock - put on black suit. Marta asked Friday - go to wake at Naval club Devonport. Mrs Tregear, ninety-one, four sons in matching ties. Someone had to carry boards in, check cards hadn't got wet, see it back in van. Kieran going too. Said all right before she finished.

Function room upstairs over bar. Carpet with anchors. Hatch - two women passed plates of sandwiches cut triangles. Sleet hit long windows, slid. Boards against wall under picture of Ark Royal. Touched the cards - dry. Four sons had two pints each by I got there. One took my hand in both said wanted me to know his mother loved way we did her hair. He said "She'd never had it like that" and cried quite suddenly, mouth open. Brother came, turned him by shoulders. Brought him chair. Didn't sit. Held back.

Kieran going round with tray of teas. Jacket off, cuffs rolled. Quick. Didn't spill. Came to me with cup. "Piotr, you want sugar?" "No." "I'll put one in anyway, you look like you need it." Did. Stirred it with small spoon ready in top pocket. Smiled. Took the cup.

Widow's sister sat by window in grey wool coat. Hands in lap. Turning ring - thin gold nearly through at back - round with thumb. Turned it whole while talked, to nobody, to window. "They said take it off her. I said no, she'll want it. Dorothy'll want it. Forty years on." I said family would have chosen. "Did she go with it?" "She did." Stood with cup and couldn't say what looking at. Kieran bending to small boy with a plate. Put plate down. Straightened. Hand in trouser pocket. Looked at picture of Ark Royal. Took it out again - only handkerchief. Nothing. Ordinary thing. Lad with handkerchief. Seen him weeks, put it away same place. Told nobody. Nobody to tell who wouldn't have to do something.

"He's a good lad," sister in grey coat said. "Very nice manner."

Loaded van dark, twenty to five. Kieran took heavy end without asking. Talked whole time - football - girlfriend's mother's caravan Looe - video man chased by swan. Shut doors. Stood moment in rain on face. "Marta said you might show me prep room properly sometime. Not tidying. Proper." I said she'd mentioned. "No rush. Only if you want." Said would see. Got in cab. He stood at passenger door a second before understood meant walk round.

Home. Blanket on radiator - dry stiff, launderette smell. Bosun on it chin on paws. Didn't get up. Pilsudski on third stair. Stood in wet suit in hall. Looked at them both. Phone went pocket again. Didn't take it out. Either hospital or wasn't. Either way decided about Tuesday and...
