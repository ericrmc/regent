# 2019-03-09

Envelope on mat, no stamp. Cream, careful biro: Mr P. Mahon. Two sides. Trewin, chartered surveyor. Fence eighteen inches over on Barry's side. Underlined. Before further steps. His phone number.

Held hands apart. Foot and a half. Width of dog lying down.

Saturday at plot. Loaded Octavia: fork, trug, Stanley, compost thawing since Thursday. Bosun back. Reg already there, flat cap, flask on water butt. "Morning Peter. You've brought your muck."

Bag split as I hauled it. Seam gave. Slow slide, black, hot-smelling. Pink worm startled. Whole thing. Held empty sleeve. "Cheap bag. Stitch them with cotton now." Shovelled it back. His knuckles size of walnuts. Knee made him crouch with one leg out. Stuff warm in middle, alive under crust.

Dug beetroot bed. Soil came up in clods with frost in undersides. Cellar door smell.

Phone rang. Audiology. Sandra. Clinic off Tuesday. Could offer 2 April, 2:10. Wrote on card in capitals: 2 APRIL 2.10. Parcel writing.
