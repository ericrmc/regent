# 2019-03-10

Window open hand's width. Warm. Kitchen smelled garden, earth, cut grass. Frost gone from shed roof, steaming in sun. Couldn't settle.

Bicycle in passage, green Dawes, forty quid off dockyard lad 1998. Back tyre flat since October. Thumb went in like bread dough. Pump in shed under bootlace. Washer gone to biscuit, broke in fingers.

Thought of market. Sunday depot yard behind tyre place. Busy. Weather brought everyone out. Wrong clothes. Pink necks. Tool man third row, grey plait, crate. Asked for pump washer. He lifted whole pump out of shoebox. Brass head, handle worn smooth as stair rail. "Two quid. Done nothing. Sat." Good clean thump on push, sigh on pull. Could have cried over pump. It had weight.

Bought a book. Paperback, no jacket, hawks. Coffee ring where opened most. Bird is flown at a weight, weight is the control, because a bird that is not hungry does not return. Diagram scales, table weights ounces. Put it back face down. Don't know why careful.

Pump worked. Tyre fat and tight. Whole day ahead and I'd done the only thing needed.
