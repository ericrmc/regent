# 2019-03-18

Window frosted in ferns. Bosun on shins. Cooker cold as tombstone. Tea in Argyle mug, chip on rim. Green flask on sill, rattling. Cupboard door on table—one screw left on second dowel. Hung it on one good hinge, stayed shut. Left third dowel alone. Washer dead.

Pillowcase by back door: sheets, towels, four white shirts. Friday's smelled of prep room. Ten to seven, went out with it over my shoulder.

Launderette past the chemist. Woman in blue tabard: "Ten or fourteen?" "Fourteen." "Number six, the seven's got a temper."

In Friday shirt pocket: card, soft at corners. Derriford Hospital. Audiology. Thursday 21st March, 10.20. Name misspelled—Mahone with the e.

Remembered it. The fifteenth—Roisin's birthday, March 15th. It was Friday. Chapel of rest with lily in each hand, arguing with florist about the ribbon. Hadn't thought of it once. Went home and had eggs.

Someone on the ladder said a name. Graham. Now it's in my head and won't leave.

Shop next door opens at eight. Card with blue jug, plain. Plastic flask, cheap, red cap, £4.49. Unscrewed it and smelled inside—nothing.

Wrote on bench: "Many happy returns, sorry it's late. P." Scratched out "Hope the boiler's behaving." Wrote it again without the P. Couldn't tell. Put P back.

Posted it in the box on the corner. Small dry sound going down.

Filled the flask from the urn at the yard. Sat on the stairs at noon with the sausage roll. Poured tea, steam going up in the cold stairwell. Four forty-nine. Couldn't get over it.
