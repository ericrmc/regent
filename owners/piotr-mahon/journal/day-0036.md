# 2019-04-08

Frost on the Corsa's roof, no, on the Octavia, this is April and I keep getting the cars wrong. Hard white until ten. Kettle, then stood at the back door with the tea and the whole yard was furred, Barry's fence too, every board outlined. Bosun went out, looked at the grass, came back in.

The cupboard by the cooker won't shut. Top hinge has pulled out of the wood and the door hangs open like a mouth. Got the biscuit tin down, the Jacob's one, full of screws. Tipped some on the table. Rust and brass and a few of Con's, the old slotted ones, all mixed. Nothing the right length. Shook it and it sounded like rain on the water butt. Left the lot on the table and the door open. Wafer sat in the cupboard.

Funeral at eleven, a Mr Hollis from Peverell, big turnout, one bearer short so I took the foot end on the left, neck complaining by the church door. Marta did the words at the graveside and I stood behind her with my hands folded. Wake at the Foresters. Ham sandwiches curling at the corners. A woman in a green coat held my sleeve and told me about his allotment and I said I had one too. She said, then you'll know. I don't know what I was meant to know.

Drove the hearse back on my own with the heater roaring.
