# 2019-03-29

Twenty to six. Tea, thumb on the chip. Cupboard door swung open. Beer mat on the lino, Bass one, gone soft. Put it back. Will be on the floor tomorrow. Hinges want a screw and wood.

Bosun at back door with his nose on the gap. Pilsudski on the third stair. Carried the dog down past him.

Ten past eight. Someone hammered the blue door. Parcel the size of a shoebox. Yellow jacket. "Coombe?" "This is not forty-one." I said Coombe was next door but one. "Are you sure?" Said I'd lived here seven years. "Right, cheers mate. It's this bloody thing." Shook the machine. Van left with a smell of diesel.

Letter on the mat. Brown envelope, window, Derriford. Name typed with the Mahon spelt right. Didn't open it. Put it in my inside pocket against my ribs.

Ten o'clock cancelled. Family moved to Monday. Cousin from Leeds. Marta wrote it in red.

Multi-storey Level 6. Parked by the barrier. Took out the envelope. New time. Tuesday in April. Twenty to ten. Fifteen minutes early, wear loose clothing. Second sheet - map with red arrow and YOU. Read it twice.

Then the hail. Grey wall from the Tamar side. Struck the roof so hard said something aloud. Bounced on the bonnet in white beads. Gulls all up at once. Noise tremendous. Ninety seconds perhaps. Ceased as if turned off. Beads on concrete with light through them. Man came out of stairwell with carrier bag over his head, laughing, stopped, looked at sky, went back. Found I was smiling.

Barber on the Plain. Stelios. Cut my hair since ninety-nine. Never asks what I'd like. Chair next to me - man my age in work fleece with plaster on thumb. Talking to mirror. Barn. Oak frame. Near Yelverton. Lifted whole thing. "You bore the hole in the post, right, and then in the tenon you bore it again but you put it a shade nearer the shoulder, a quarter inch, so the peg's got to bend to go through, and as it goes in it drags the joint tight. That's a drawbore pin. Four hundred years they'll stand." Thought of the shoehorn. Hole at its end.

Flower woman not at market. Remembered was Marta's job.

Removal from Stoke. Eighty-eight. Bungalow. Photograph of a ship on every wall. Wife said, "He was in the Navy. He never talked about it." Brought her a chair. Said we'd be as quick and gentle as we could. Kieran at the head. Hands.

Dennis at garage door with his cap in his fist. "Piotr, Piotr." Took my hand. Held it longer. Waited. "Nice haircut." Thanks. "Stelios?" Yes. "Good man." Looked at the Mercedes. That was all.

Six o'clock. Quiet. Four pierogi left, pan with butter. Stood at counter with fork. Ate one, two, three, fourth going cold while looked at the cupboard. Door come open again. Beer mat on lino. Dust ring where it'd been.

Picked up the mat. Thought about drawer with screws and piece of wood. How long - ten minutes. Peg might...
