# 2019-03-15

Window clear. Frost white line on the fence tops. Hedge black and separate.

Cupboard swung out an inch slowly, like it was only resting its eyes.

Bag of compost by the back door—seam went down one side. 40 litres. Black tongue coming out, damp, bits of bark, white worm of something. Sweet, cold, cellar smell. Stood holding the empty top half like a man handed a coat. Wafer sniffed and walked off. Pilsudski watched from the third stair.

Dustpan, coal shovel, on my knees. Compost goes into the lino joints, won't come out. Washing-up bowl. Bucket. Four trips. Kept back a cup for the window box. Hands black to the wrist, smelled like good part of a garden.

Barry started next door—hammer drill, on and off, long gaps. Seven o'clock, near enough. Comes through the party wall as a shudder. Cups answered on the rack.

Stood at the back door with black hands like a surgeon's. Nobody there.

Washing machine man rang—young, hoarse. Can come Monday 8-12 instead of end of month. Said I'd be at work. He said needed somebody there, to talk about it. Said I'd get somebody. Said Tuesday 8-12. Said Monday.

Afternoon had a hole in it. Funeral at two—94-year-old from a home. Six people, vicar with a cold. Light so hard the brass handles threw stars on the ceiling. Vicar sneezed at the committal, apologized to the coffin. Nephew's hands in his pockets the whole way. Shook my hand after. "You're the only one who looked at us."

Three thirty, stairwell, sausage roll gone leathery. Three bites like something posted.

Drove home the long way. Sun orange behind the Hoe. Sea flat and hard as a plate. Barbican windows on fire. Stopped at the wall. Hands in my armpits. No wind. Two gulls on a bollard, not speaking. Dialled. Crack in screen catching the sun. Rang 14 times. Tone. Woman I didn't know. Number not available. Thought they'd turned the phone off.

Pulled the cupboard door off the hinges. Laid it on the table. Chipboard round the hinge plate gone to cake. Hole the size of my little finger. Won't hold a screw. Carried it to the hall, leaned it against the wall next to the letter.

Cupboard stood open on its tins. Beans, tomatoes, three tins of pink fish, tin of pears Mum liked. Looked at that a good while.

Kettle on. Took Barry's green flask down. Filled it. Stood with the lid in my hand, waiting for it warm enough to say I'd tried. Drill next door started again. Three short, one long. Cups shook. Thought if I go round now with the screwdriver, he'll think I've come about the fence.
