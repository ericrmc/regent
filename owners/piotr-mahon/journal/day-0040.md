# 2019-04-12

Hard frost, white to the gutters until ten. Bosun wouldn't go out past the step, so I carried him to the grass and stood there in my socks like a fool. Boots stiff as boards.

A delivery driver at the yard gate before eight, lorry idling, wanting Voisey Joinery. Told him that's Devonport, and he looked at me as if I'd moved it. Left the gate open behind him and the cold came right down the stairwell.

The strip light on the middle landing has been flickering since Christmas. Alan's step ladder, the one with a wobble, one foot short. I held it for Kieran while he changed the tube. Bad neck, my whole face turned away. Someone upstairs had a radio on. A mazurka, thin through the fire door, the one Halina hummed over the sink. Gone before I placed it.
