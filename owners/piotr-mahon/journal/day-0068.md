# day 68

Day 68. Bad one. Up 5:40, tea, chip under the thumb. Bosun bottom stair, Pilsudski third, carried him, said nothing to either. Van slow. Marta in the doorway again, I looked at the mop. Sausage roll, Stoke Damerel, cold. Two removals, the second one a boy of nineteen, so I brought the chair and said nothing to the mother for a long while. Afterwards the tea round the back. Read the tail of the code after eggs, it fits on a page and a bit. Asked it to count what it reads. Wafer on the lid. Nula Sunday, bus 34. Landing, maybe. Not tonight.
