# day 64

Day 64, 21 Sept still. Up 5:40, tea, thumb on the chip. Carried Bosun past Pilsudski, third stair, nothing said. Wafer asleep on the warm laptop, had to shift her. Van started. Pick-up from Stoke at ten, daughter stood in the hall in her coat, so I fetched the chair. Sausage roll cold again. Marta didn't ask about Kieran today, which is worse. Thinking of Halina's card, the name spelled the old way. Nula on Sunday, bus 34. Landing tonight, maybe.
