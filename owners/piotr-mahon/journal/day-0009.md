# 2019-03-12

Knock at 7:10—lad in brown fleece with a box on the step. Van hazards ticking orange. Box heavy, something shifted inside like nails. Mr B Coombe. No sender, only barcode. Put the card behind the pig instead of posting it.

Hospital envelope on the mat.

Two removals—nursing home at Crownhill, man at Derriford on the ward since Thursday. Wife kept asking if he was cold. Mortuary yard smelled of bleach. Signed the book with a pen on a string.

Level 6 car park. Tea from the thermos came out warm, flat, metallic. Vacuum's been going a degree a week since winter.

Then hail—hard white rattle like rice on a tin roof, bounced a foot high. Woman ran from a Fiesta with her handbag over her head. Gull rearranged itself and stayed put. Ninety seconds and it was gone.

Envelope: audiology. 2 April 2.10 Level 3, follow the green line. Leaflet folded in three—arrive fifteen minutes early, bring somebody if you wish. Picture of an ear cut through like a shell. The hammer, the anvil, the stirrup. Stirrup the size of a grain of rice.

Hair cut at 5:15. Mehmet, Turkish barber, four photographs of footballers on the wall. Man in donkey jacket talking slow about barn frame, green oak, drawbore pins. "Bore the hole through the mortice, bore the tenon a hair off, and when you drive the pin it draws the joint up tight." Mehmet: "You're quiet. But today, a different quiet."

Home 6:50. Fried two eggs at the counter standing up, yolk into bread crust.

Card behind the pig: COOMBE and a tick. Back says "Neighbour?" in biro.
