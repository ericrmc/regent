# 2019-03-08

Frost thick. Green light on phone. Chloe confirming Monday 11, arrive 10 minutes early. Cupboard door hung true.

Windscreen grey lace. Scraped with library card. Compost in boot hard as gravel now, frozen through.

Kieran at yard with bucket warm water from tap. Trick his nan taught. Poured over hearse roof, wiped with chamois. Steamed, then just clean black. Did the wing mirrors well.

Marta had daughter in front room. Fifty, camel coat collar up. Brother beside, walking stick between knees, both hands top. Wrong on sofa. I got upright chair from arrangements room. He looked as if I'd offered a drink, then moved and sat straight.

Stripped duvet off bed without thinking, dog beds, cats' blanket. None goes in machine. Took all to launderette on Alma Road. Hot. Woman in tabard folding sheets, corners in teeth. Lad asleep on bench.

Found appointment card in coat pocket. Derriford. Audiology. Tuesday 12 March 9:40. Carrying it since February. Told no one.

Roisin's birthday the sixth. Two days late. Called her. Line went dead. Four minutes eleven. Door somewhere, murmur, voices.
