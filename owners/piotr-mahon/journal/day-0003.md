# 2019-03-06

Twenty-two hours on the matchsticks. Bosun stops at third stair where Pilsudski sleeps. Compost still in the blue crate.

Hammer next door. Barry on the fence. Tock. Pause. Tock. Through the bare gap where the hedge had been, top of his head. If it's the line he says it is, there's a nail in it now.

Vet rang again. Chloe. Could do Monday 11 or Tuesday the week after. I said Monday. Did the dog eat all right? He ate a sock in 2017. She laughed—real laugh.

Didn't take the dog. Compost in black bin bags. Held the split shut with my forearm. Put it in the boot. Rolled when I shut the door, settled with soft push, like something sighing.

Mrs Rudd's removed. Daughter rang at seven, got a date she likes better. Took myself to prep room instead—steel, drain, tiles. Favourite job. Ran the hose. Hollow sound.
