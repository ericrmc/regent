# 2019-03-14

Sleet on the glass like throwing sugar. Foghorn gone in the night, left a hole.

Washing machine stopped mid-cycle. Drum full of grey water. Sheets came out cold, heavy as a drowned man. Beer mat on the floor again.

Letter on the shelf since Monday. Three thousand nine hundred pounds for the Octavia in full and final settlement. Confirm by the 28th. Car sitting at Ernesettle with its side pushed in.

Funeral at eleven—Leonard Hosking, 81, electrician in the dockyard forty years. Wife Doreen, two sons, grandson tall for his suit. Efford chapel, then the club at Stoke.

Marta in the yard: "Are you all right on the left side? You're carrying high."

Chapel smelled of floor polish and lilies. Eldest son read something about a ship, head down. Shoes polished the night before, left lace in a double knot. Younger one had a tear across the toe.

Man at the club, ninety if he was a day. "Con's boy. I was on the Ark Royal refit with him. Big lad. Never wore gloves." Cuff damp where his hand had been.

Phone at 2:30—audiology. Clinic consolidated. Can offer 30th April, half nine, Level 3. Took it. Four more weeks of turning my head on the pillow. Written the 2nd on the calendar in pen above the pig. Will cross it out.

Launderette at the bottom of the Plain. Raleigh blue against the window, back tyre flat on the kerb. Lad in delivery jacket asking for a pump. Valve the thin sort with a brass nut. Pumped at an angle. Air hissed out. "Mate, honestly. Only up the road." Walked it away tipped back on the front wheel.

Warm inside, smelled of hot lint. Machine number six. Sat with the letter on my knee. Dear Sirs. Regarding claim number.

Man across from me, sixty, hands like shovels. "Forty years I did it. Bridges. Saltash. Hammer, little one, go along the steel and tap it. If the note's dull you know there's something hollow behind it. A void." Held his hand out and knocked on his own knee twice. Like a knock on a door.

Seven o'clock from the step under the awning. Rang her eleven times. Thought of her getting up out of the chair one hip at a time. Call ended.

Sheets warm and heavy from the dryer. Dog blanket smell—still faintly of dog, like a room where someone's just left.
